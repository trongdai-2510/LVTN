# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Song Thanh has two user groups. Internal users are content editors, catalog managers, sales support, and administrators who need a calm, reliable admin workspace for website content, brand pages, product data, files, contact requests, and quote requests. Public users are contractors, buyers, and business customers who need to quickly understand what Song Thanh provides, browse product and brand information, and contact the company for advice or quotation.

## Product Purpose

The system is a company website with structured content and product data management. It helps Song Thanh present trustworthy business information, maintain reusable product and brand data, reduce repeated manual explanation, and let internal staff update content without developer help. Success means public information is clear and verifiable, admin workflows are hard to misuse, and unpublished, inactive, or unverified data never leaks to the public site.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, organized. The interface should feel like a serious B2B technical workspace: clear hierarchy, restrained confidence, and enough polish that users trust the data without feeling distracted by decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid ecommerce-heavy patterns such as carts, checkout language, fake discounts, stock urgency, and product-marketplace density. Avoid generic AI-looking layouts, oversized decorative cards, gradient-heavy SaaS pages, unsupported claims such as "official distributor" without source, and any section that implies strategic partnership or project usage without verified internal data.

## Design Principles

1. Make the content workflow obvious: editors should know what they can edit here, what belongs in Brand, Banner, or Product modules, and what affects the public site.
2. Separate verified public content from internal working notes, especially for claims, stats, sources, and publishing.
3. Use structured entity data for long-term content, not loose generic pages when the content belongs to a brand, service, project, or product.
4. Keep public pages polished but sober: real data, clear routes, no empty sections, no fake imagery, no unsupported marketing claims.
5. Prefer safe defaults: inactive brands, draft pages, unpublished products, and unverified stats stay hidden publicly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Vietnamese UI copy, predictable controls, strong focus states, responsive layouts, and semantic page structure. Admin screens should favor clarity for non-technical operators over technical shortcuts.
